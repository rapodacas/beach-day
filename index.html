🗺️ The Plan

Coastal escape while Escondido bakes. Built flat & crutch-friendly — no soft-sand marches.

Top pick

Del Mar — Powerhouse Park + beachfront lunch

Flat grass & paved paths sitting right above the sand — ocean breeze, benches, no terrain to fight. Lunch on the beach at Jake's Del Mar or Poseidon (ramp/level access, ocean-view tables).

Backup

Carlsbad Seawall + Park 101

One of the flattest oceanfront walks in the county (Tamarack → Village). Eat at Park 101 (casual, rooftop, fun for the girls) or Beach House on the sand.

🦽 The move that changes everything: Del Mar, Carlsbad & Oceanside loan free beach wheelchairs at the lifeguard stations. Reserve one so she can actually get onto the sand with everyone — not stuck on the pavement. Call ahead the morning of.
